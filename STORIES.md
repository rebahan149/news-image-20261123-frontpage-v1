## Heroes (drawn, 1–3)

1. **Santa Clara, USA — Nvidia reinstates Jensen Huang.** Nvidia
   said Monday it was reinstating Huang as CEO, effective
   immediately, 11 days after he resigned amid revelations of
   extramarital affairs, including with an alleged Chinese spy.
   Shares surged more than 31% (premarket/close per Reuters);
   Nasdaq +6.5%, S&P 500 +4.7% (Reuters Trading Day). Trump welcomed
   the move ("I made the deal", "the correct and proper decision").
   The US move to blacklist Asian AI firm Megaspeed (BBC) is
   mentioned in the summary; the BBC item itself is timestamped
   Tuesday 06:15 GMT, so Megaspeed is not a numbered story and the
   blacklist is noted in the DATE CHECK as surfacing Tuesday.
   Sources: Reuters (three items, Mon 23 Nov), BBC.
2. **Kyiv / Benghazi — Ukraine strikes Russian corvette in Libya.**
   Ukraine said its naval drones hit the Russian corvette Merkury
   at Benghazi port; Zelensky confirmed and strike video was
   released Monday (BBC, 19:34 GMT). The cargo ship Angara was also
   struck (BBC reporting via news summaries). Libyan authorities
   and the UN condemned the strike (Arab News bulletin, Mon 18:00
   GMT). Labelled OFFICIAL: the claim is Kyiv's, made on the record
   with footage; the sinking itself is not independently verified.
   Sources: BBC, Al Jazeera / Arab News.
3. **Brussels, Belgium — national strike grounds flights.** Air
   traffic controllers (skeyes) joined a nationwide day of union
   action against austerity called by ACV-CSC, ABVV-FGTB and
   Synova. Brussels Airport cancelled all arrivals/departures
   07:00–21:00 local: 429 flights and ~59,500 passengers scheduled;
   Charleroi cancelled all flights for the day. Rail (SNCB) largely
   ran. Sources: BBC (Mon 21:34 GMT), Al Jazeera (skeyes statement),
   Brussels Airport via aviation press.

## Grid panels (drawn, 4–11)

4. **Tehran, Iran — singer flogged.** Parastoo Ahmadi and four
   members of her production team each received 74 lashes, plus
   two-year bans on artistic/musical activity and travel, after her
   hijab-less performance video; sentence reported Monday by the
   Emtedad website via her lawyer Mostafa Nili. Sources: Reuters
   (Mon 23 Nov), BBC.
5. **New Delhi, India — $1.6bn for Nepal.** India pledged $1.6bn to
   Nepal as PM Balendra Shah visited Delhi: flood relief after
   deadly floods, port access and a new train service, with
   contentious issues set aside. Source: BBC (Mon 21:39 GMT).
6. **Cikande, Indonesia — factory fire toll confirmed at 24.**
   Indonesia confirmed Monday that 24 people died in the fire at
   the PT Fonusa Agung Mulia (United Family Food) confectionery
   factory in Cikande, Banten; about 30 still missing, search
   continuing in the unstable wreckage; preliminary cause an
   air-conditioner short circuit. The fire itself broke out
   Saturday evening — Monday's story is the confirmed toll (see
   DATE CHECK). Sources: Reuters (Mon 23 Nov), Al Jazeera, BBC,
   The Guardian.
7. **London, UK — Capita crash.** Capita CEO Adolfo Hernandez
   stepped down with immediate effect Monday (announced 07:00)
   over the Civil Service Pension Scheme failures; CFO Pablo
   Andres took over. Shares fell ~70% to 72p, the lowest since
   October 1991; 2027 operating profit cut by £85–95m; a £190m
   equity raise planned for H1 2027. Sources: The Guardian / PA,
   Reuters, The Register.
8. **London, UK — Burnham's UK–EU deal.** PM Andy Burnham took his
   UK–EU deal to the Commons on Monday, saying rejoining would be
   "unthinkable" without a referendum; former PMs including Rishi
   Sunak attacked the move and the Conservatives vowed to scrap
   the deal. Source: BBC (live coverage, Mon).
9. **Washington, USA — $1bn Binance recovery.** The Justice
   Department filed a civil action Monday to recover more than
   $1 billion in cryptocurrency stolen by North Korean cyber
   actors in a hack of Binance. Source: Reuters (Mon 23 Nov).
10. **Austin, USA — CrowdStrike buys Zscaler.** CrowdStrike agreed
    to acquire Zscaler in an all-stock deal worth about $44bn
    (Reuters: $44bn; CNBC: ~$43.9bn), among the largest
    cybersecurity deals ever. Sources: Reuters (Mon 23 Nov), CNBC.
11. **Nairobi / Kinshasa — Kenya–DRC ties.** Kenya and DR Congo
    agreed to restore ties after tensions over the conflict in
    Congo's east. Source: BBC (Mon 16:53 GMT).
